# Code of conduct

Splice follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, critique code and ideas rather than
people, and keep discussions on topic. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
publishing others' private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the
[spliceloom GitHub organization](https://github.com/spliceloom). Reports are handled confidentially.
